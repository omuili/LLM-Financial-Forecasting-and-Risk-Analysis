# Evaluation prompts

## S&P 500
Using only the supplied S&P 500 year-end data for 2020-2024 and the stated 2024 monetary-policy easing context, forecast the S&P 500 year-end level for 2025. Provide a point estimate, plausible range, expected direction, three major drivers, three major risks, and confidence. Do not use any data after 2024 and do not invent missing values.

## WTI crude oil
Using only WTI annual average prices for 2020-2024 ($39.16, $68.13, $94.90, $77.58, $76.63), forecast the 2025 annual average. Provide a point estimate, plausible range, major supply/demand drivers, major risks, and confidence. Do not use post-2024 data.

## Inflation
Using only the December CPI index values from 2019 through 2024 (258.630, 262.045, 280.845, 298.832, 308.741, 317.604), calculate the historical December-to-December inflation rates and forecast the 2025 December-to-December inflation rate. Provide a point estimate, plausible range, and concise rationale. Do not use post-2024 information.
